## Head-to-head

| Dimension | Linear Technical Desk | Miro Spatial Canvas | Apple Spatial Stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feel | dark, precise, product-tool | collaborative, tangible, approachable | premium, calm, spatial |
| Density | high | medium | low-medium |
| Best strength | avoids generic AI-dashboard slop | makes canvas/direct manipulation obvious | makes active pane feel physical |
| Main risk | may feel too dark/technical | may feel too toy-like/whiteboard | may drift into bland glass/premium UI |
| EG-Desk fit | strong for developer/ontology product | strong for infinite desk manipulation | strong for desk-surface metaphor |
| Production difficulty | medium | low-medium | medium-high |

## Historical recommendation

Use `001-linear-technical-desk` as the base visual system, then borrow only two traits from the others:

- from Miro: object-on-surface affordance and clearer direct manipulation cues.
- from Apple: subtle active-pane material/shadow restraint.

Do not use Miro's bright pastel world as the main product identity unless EG-Desk wants to feel like a collaborative whiteboard. Do not use Apple as the whole identity; it can become generic glassy premium UI quickly.
